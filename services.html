Services

Full-Spectrum Book Marketing Services For Serious Authors

From Amazon visibility to reader community building — every service is documented, data-driven, and tailored to your genre.

1. Amazon Listing & SEO Optimization

Your Amazon listing is your storefront — if it's not optimized, even the best ads won't save it. I rebuild your listing from the ground up to maximize both visibility and conversion.

  • Full keyword research using real Amazon search data
  • Backend keyword optimization (hidden fields)
  • Category & subcategory bestseller positioning
  • Book description rewrite for search + conversion
  • Title/subtitle optimization recommendations
  • A/B testing recommendations for covers & copy
Ideal for: Any published author whose book isn't ranking or converting the way it should.

2. Amazon Ads (PPC) Management

Professionally managed paid advertising that puts your book directly in front of buyers, with full transparency on spend and results.

  • Sponsored Products setup (Auto + Manual targeting)
  • Sponsored Brands setup for series authors
  • Keyword bid strategy & ongoing optimization
  • Negative keyword management
  • Weekly performance reports by email
  • Monthly strategy adjustments from sales data
Ideal for: Authors ready to invest in paid visibility, managed professionally.

3. Book Launch Strategy & Management

A complete, structured roadmap that turns launch day chaos into a confident, step-by-step execution plan.

  • 60/30/14/7-day pre-launch roadmap
  • ARC distribution & reviewer outreach strategy
  • Launch week daily action plan via email
  • Bestseller category positioning strategy
  • Post-launch momentum plan
  • Full written timeline for reference
Ideal for: Authors preparing to launch who want a professional plan instead of guesswork.

4. Author Branding & Positioning

Build a compelling, consistent author identity that readers remember, trust, and return to for your next release.

  • Author positioning strategy within your genre
  • Bio rewrite (short, medium, long versions)
  • Core messaging document (your story, your "why")
  • Visual brand direction recommendations
  • Social profile optimization
  • Complete brand reference guide document
Ideal for: Authors building a long-term career, not just a single release.

5. Book Club & Reader Community Outreach

Genuine reader-community buzz — connecting your book with the real communities that actually discuss and recommend books in your genre.

  • Research & outreach to relevant book clubs
  • Book club discussion guide creation
  • Pitch email writing & sending to organizers
  • Bookstagram & BookTok community outreach
  • Full outreach tracking document shared with you
Ideal for: Authors who want genuine reader buzz, not just paid ad traffic.

6. PR & Media Outreach

Build credibility and visibility beyond Amazon through strategic media placements and podcast features.

  • Media list building (podcasts, blogs, press)
  • Press kit creation (bio, summary, assets)
  • Email pitch writing & outreach on your behalf
  • Podcast guest pitch outreach
  • Full outreach tracking spreadsheet
Ideal for: Authors looking to build credibility beyond Amazon alone.

7. Email List & Newsletter Marketing

Own your audience directly instead of relying entirely on Amazon or social platform algorithms.

  • Reader email list growth strategy
  • Welcome sequence copywriting (3-5 emails)
  • Launch sequence copywriting
  • Newsletter swap outreach with other authors
  • Platform setup guidance (Mailchimp, ConvertKit, etc.)
Ideal for: Authors who want to own their audience long-term.

8. Review Acquisition Strategy

Compliant, ethical strategies to build the social proof your book needs to convert browsers into buyers.

  • Compliant ARC distribution (BookSirens, NetGalley, StoryOrigin)
  • Review request email sequence for past readers
  • Goodreads review campaign strategy
  • Post-purchase review reminder system (TOS-compliant)
Ideal for: Authors struggling to build initial reviews and credibility.

9. Goodreads Optimization

Strengthen your visibility within the reader-first Goodreads community, where serious readers discover their next book.

  • Author profile setup & optimization
  • Book page optimization (shelving, genre tagging)
  • Goodreads giveaway strategy for pre-launch buzz
  • Author Q&A and update post strategy
Ideal for: Authors wanting stronger visibility in the Goodreads community.

10. Box Set & Series Marketing Strategy

Maximize the compounding value of a series instead of marketing each book in isolation.

  • Series-wide positioning strategy
  • Box set pricing & bundling strategy
  • Cross-promotion plan between books in series
  • "Read-through" optimization strategies
Ideal for: Authors with an existing series or planning to bundle titles.

11. Audiobook Marketing

Extend your book's reach into the fast-growing audiobook market with proper positioning and optimization.

  • Audiobook platform optimization (Audible, ACX)
  • Audiobook-specific marketing copy & positioning
  • Cross-promotion across ebook, print & audio formats
Ideal for: Authors with or planning an audiobook version.

12. Award Submission Strategy

Build long-term credibility and prestige through strategic book award submissions.

  • Research into relevant, credible book awards
  • Submission timeline & requirements tracking
  • Submission copywriting assistance
Ideal for: Authors looking to build long-term credibility and recognition.
