My Story

Why I Do This Work

I started in book marketing because I kept seeing the same frustrating pattern: brilliant authors writing incredible books — and then watching those books disappear into the noise of millions of other titles, simply because no one knew how to market them properly.

Writing a book is one skill. Selling a book is a completely different one. My job is to bridge that gap — turning finished manuscripts into strategically positioned products that actually reach readers.

"Over the years, I've worked with self-published authors, first-time writers, and established names across fiction and non-fiction — helping them build marketing strategies that convert real sales, not just vanity metrics."

I run my entire process over email by design. It keeps everything documented, gives you time to actually think through decisions instead of being rushed on a call, and creates a clear reference trail for every strategy, deadline, and deliverable we agree on.

My Process Philosophy

No Calls. No Meetings. Just Clear, Documented Communication.

  • Everything Is On Record

    Every strategy, deadline, and decision is searchable and referenceable, forever.

  • No Rushed Decisions

    You get time to read, think, and respond thoughtfully instead of being put on the spot.

  • No Scheduling Friction

    No time zone issues, no calendar back-and-forth, no missed calls.

  • Professional Accountability

    Written communication keeps both of us accountable to what was actually agreed.
